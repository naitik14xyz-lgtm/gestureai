How It Works

Six-stage computer vision pipeline running locally in the browser.

1. Camera InputWebcam frames via getUserMedia
2. Hand DetectionMediaPipe HandLandmarker
3. 3D Landmarks21 skeletal key points
4. Feature ExtractionPIP/DIP joint angles & ratios
5. ClassificationRule engine + 9-frame smoothing
6. App ActionSlides, Media, IoT, Speech, Draw
